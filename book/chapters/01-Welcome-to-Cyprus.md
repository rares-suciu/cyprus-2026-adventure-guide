# Welcome to Cyprus

> **Trip:** 26 October – 5 November 2026  
> **Base:** Protaras

## Why Cyprus?

Cyprus combines warm Mediterranean waters, dramatic rocky coastlines, excellent snorkeling, mountain villages, archaeological sites, and outstanding food—all within short driving distances.

Late October is one of the best periods to visit:
- Warm sea
- Pleasant daytime temperatures
- Fewer crowds
- Better prices
- Comfortable sightseeing weather

## What Makes This Guide Different?

This isn't a list of attractions.

It's an adventure guide built around daily missions, hidden gems, and memorable family experiences.

Every recommendation is personally selected using these criteria:

- 🌊 Water quality
- 🤿 Snorkeling
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Family friendly
- 📷 Photography
- 🚗 Easy parking
- 🍽️ Great food nearby
- ⭐ Overall experience
